# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint. Participation should be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Help newcomers understand the project and its contribution process.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sustained personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, or bad-faith participation
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and public interactions representing the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer using the contact information on the [@YMuskrat GitHub profile](https://github.com/YMuskrat). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of the community.
